What do you need help with?

Quick protocols

Progression

When you can complete every set with clean technique and still have roughly 1–2 good reps in reserve, add a small number of reps or progress to a harder variation.

Recovery

Rest 60–120 seconds between ordinary strength sets, sleep consistently, hydrate, and avoid training through sharp pain.

Consistency

Start with a schedule you can repeat. Two or three well-spaced sessions are better than doing too much in week one and stopping.
