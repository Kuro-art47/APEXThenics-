PROGRESSION RULES

  • Finish sets with roughly 1–3 good reps left; technique comes first.
  • When every set reaches the top of its rep range with clean form for two sessions, progress slightly.
  • If performance drops sharply or recovery is poor, reduce work or rest.
  • Allow challenging muscle groups time to recover.
